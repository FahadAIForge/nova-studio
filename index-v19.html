Our Story · Bean to Cup

From a hillside in Yirgacheffe.

Washed process, 1,900 metres up. The cherries are picked by hand, dried on raised beds, and shipped to our little roastery — where twelve kilos at a time become your morning cup.

One farm, one roast.

We buy the whole harvest of a single washing station in Yirgacheffe. When it is gone, it is gone — the next lot comes next season, and it will taste a little different. That is the point.

1,900m

Altitude

High-grown, slow-ripening cherries. Denser beans, sweeter cup.

12kg

Batch size

Twelve kilos at a time. Small enough to taste every batch.

18h

Cold brew steep

Eighteen slow hours for the cold brew. No heat, no rush.

95°C

Brew temp

Water at 95 degrees. Hot enough to open the bean, cool enough to keep it sweet.

How it works · Four steps

Source. Roast. Brew. Pour.

Four cards, four steps. Scroll and each one peels away to the next — the same way the bean becomes the cup.

01

Source

Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia

Washed process, hand-picked cherries from one washing station, dried on raised beds under the highland sun.

02

Roast

Twelve kilos, one drum

Roasted in small batches at golden hour. Light is floral, medium is caramel, dark is cocoa — you choose the wheel.

03

Brew

95 degrees, no rush

Water at 95°C, ground fresh for the method — espresso in seconds, pour-over in slow spirals, cold brew in eighteen hours.

04

Pour

Into your cup

Into the takeaway paper cup, kraft sleeve, glossy lid — or over ice in the clear glass. Take it home, or drink it on the walk.
